Our Story

Founded in 1998 by artisan and entrepreneur Reza Mehran, our company began with a deep passion for the art of Persian carpet weaving. Reza, a third-generation carpet expert from Tabriz, Iran, grew up surrounded by the textures and traditions of handwoven rugs. Inspired by his family's heritage and a desire to share this cultural treasure with the world, he set out to build a business rooted in authenticity and craftsmanship.

Starting with just a handful of carefully chosen pieces at local markets, Reza's vision grew into a global platform for genuine Persian rugs. Today, we continue his legacy with the same commitment to quality and tradition that shaped our beginning.

Farshestan's Life
Journey

1945

Reza Mehran was born in Iran, where the art of rug weaving was part of daily life. From an early age, he was captivated by the textures, colors, and stories that each carpet carried.

1963

As a young man, Reza began selling Persian carpets in Iran’s bustling markets. He earned a reputation for his keen eye and deep respect for craftsmanship passed down through generations.

1980

With a vision to share Persian heritage with the world, Reza moved to the UK. Starting from scratch, he sold carpets in market stalls and small retail spaces, introducing Persian tradition to a new audience.

1998

Reza officially established the brand, rooted in a mission to preserve the soul of Persian rug-making and elevate the voices of the artisans behind each piece.

2001

The first flagship store opened its doors in London, offering not just rugs, but a connection to history, artistry, and culture.

2008

The legend leaves us, but his dream lives on. A legacy of craftsmanship, culture, and connection that continues to inspire generations. His sons took the helm, carrying his vision forward with deep respect and fresh ambition.

2012

The brand took Persian carpets global by launching its e-commerce platform, reaching customers everywhere and transforming how timeless craftsmanship is discovered and loved.

Handcrafted Heritage

We create our rugs from the original works of Reza Mehran, made before his passing. His artistic vision lives on through these works, and new designs continue to be hand-painted in the same spirit today. Inspired by nature, they feature deep reds, pink roses, purple irises and lilies, combined with floral patterns, stylised Islamic motifs and narrative elements. Each rug reflects a balance of tradition and timeless design, turning every piece into a visual story.

Handwoven in Iran

Every rug we sell is handwoven in Iran using traditional methods passed down through generations. Skilled artisans work with natural wool, silk and vegetable dyes to produce pieces full of colour and character. Some rugs take months to complete, with thousands of knots tied by hand. Each one is a unique expression of craftsmanship, culture and artistry.
